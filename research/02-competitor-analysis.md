## Patterns of the Top 10%

These five patterns appear on every winning Houma site and are missing from the weakest ones.

### 1. A trust stack above the fold.
Every leader shows credentials within the first viewport. Cleaner Image stacks Angelwax / Dr. Colorchip / IDA logos. Gladiator shows "Licensed & Insured." Power Wash Pro shows "Family-Owned · Locally-Owned · Insured." Tru Wash needs the same: a 3-badge row directly under the hero showing **Owner-Operated · Houma-Born · Insured.**

### 2. Owner story humanizes the brand.
Gladiator's Matthew LeBlanc page is the strongest example — face, name, years in the business, why they started. Cleaner Image runs a "since 2000" line as a less-developed version of the same play. Tru Wash should run a "Meet Tory" slot with portrait + 3-paragraph story. (We'll mark a placeholder until the client sends a photo.)

### 3. Named-person testimonials with star rows.
Power Wash Pro's 30+ named, dated, 5-star reviews are the single biggest reason they out-rank similar pressure-wash sites. Tru Wash should embed a 3-up testimonial grid pulling from real Facebook comments (with the customer's first name + initial). Five testimonials minimum, named, with stars rendered as SVG, not emojis.

### 4. Package pricing or starting-at floors.
Bayou Details publishes $174.99 / $229.99 / $329.99. This eliminates the "are they expensive?" hesitation and pre-qualifies leads. Tru Wash should show "Starting at $X" on each service card — even a soft floor like "Detail packages from $149" sets the expectation and converts.

### 5. Clear bundle/discount story for multi-service shops.
Advanced Soft Wash hints at bundled discounts. Nobody does this loudly. Tru Wash's biggest structural advantage is offering **detail + lawn + pressure wash from one crew** — the site should lead with a "Bundle and save" panel that no competitor has. This is the wedge.

## Bonus Pattern — what NONE of them do well

- **No competitor has a dual-service-track navigation.** Detail and lawn are different decision moments for the homeowner. Tru Wash can split the homepage into a clear "For your vehicle" and "For your property" rhythm, each with its own accent color (water blue / spring green) sharing a navy backbone. This visual division is what Houma's market is missing.
- **No competitor uses scroll-driven animation.** Every site is static. A premium hero with scroll-triggered reveal of services will make Tru Wash feel a tier above on the very first scroll.
- **No competitor has a downloadable price sheet or instant-quote calculator.** Out of scope for v1 but worth flagging.

## SEO Landscape

### Keywords competitors rank for
- "auto detailing houma" (Cleaner Image, Bayou Details)
- "mobile car detailing houma" (Bayou Details)
- "pressure washing houma" (Power Wash Pro, Advanced Soft Wash)
- "lawn care houma" (Gladiator, Duplantis)
- "ceramic coating houma" (Cleaner Image)

### Keyword gaps Tru Wash should target
- **"detail and lawn service houma"** — nobody owns this combined query
- **"mobile detail and pressure wash houma"** — combo gap
- **"houma car detail and yard service"** — combo gap
- **"Terrebonne Parish detailing"** — secondary geo
- **"affordable detail houma"** — aligns with "cheaper prices, better work" motto

### Schema markup
Every leader uses LocalBusiness schema. Tru Wash will use `Service` schema for each service offering + `LocalBusiness` for the parent entity. Embed phone, area served (Houma, Terrebonne Parish), hours, and aggregate rating once the client supplies real reviews.
