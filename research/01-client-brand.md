## 6. Tone of Voice
Pulled from three signals:
- Motto "Cheaper prices better work!" — direct, working-class, confident about value.
- FB profile graphic citing Genesis 3:19 — humble, faith-influenced.
- Review snippets: "very kind and polite", "best in Houma in auto detailing", "easy to work with."

**Voice rules for site copy:**
- Plain talk. No corporate jargon. No "leverage", no "solutions".
- Confident, not boastful. "Best in Houma" can be quoted from a customer, not claimed by us.
- Short sentences. Honest about what's included.
- Warm where appropriate. Houma is a tight community.
